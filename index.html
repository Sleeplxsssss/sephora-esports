What the integration does

Simple notifications for public TikTok videos

Community purpose

Sephora E-Sports is a Rocket League E-Sports community with an official Discord server and a TikTok presence.

Authorized access only

TikTok access is used only after explicit OAuth authorization by the Sephora E-Sports TikTok account.

Public video detection

The integration checks for new public TikTok videos from the authorized account and posts a notice in the official Discord server.

No video publishing

The application does not publish TikTok videos, upload content, or post to TikTok on behalf of the account.

Data and platform notes

No tracking on this website

This static website uses no analytics, no advertising trackers, and no cookies unless a future hosting platform requires strictly technical cookies outside this website's control.
